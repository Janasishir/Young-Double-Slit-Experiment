Newton's Second Law

The net force acting on an object is related to its acceleration.

F = ma

F = force, m = mass, a = acceleration.

🧲

Ohm's Law

For an ohmic conductor under suitable conditions:

V = IR

V = voltage, I = current, R = resistance.

🌀

Quantum Energy

A photon has energy proportional to its frequency:

E = hf

h = Planck's constant, f = frequency.
